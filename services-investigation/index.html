Noise Investigation and Remediation

From the Sound Up provides noise investigation and remediation consulting services in Winnipeg, across Canada and internationally.

I work with a diverse range of project partners to develop and achieve acoustical goals, including property owners and developers, cultural and civic institutions, architects, and interior designers. I often help project owners shape acoustical goals before a project has even begun through workshops, visioning studies and real estate guidance.

I have provided acoustical consulting services for a broad range of sectors, including:

  • Performing Arts
  • Museums
  • Civic Centres
  • Churches and Houses of Worship
  • Schools and Universities
  • Commercial Offices
  • Retail
  • Restaurants and Hospitality
  • Airports and Transport Hubs
  • Multi-Family Residential and Condos
  • Mixed-Use Buildings
  • Private Residences
